Generated by `scripts/build_dataset.py`. Do not edit by hand.

OpenF1 lists 70 Grands Prix in 2023-2025 (it has no data before 2023; the cancelled 2023 Emilia Romagna GP is not counted). 57 are used.

A race is left out if: it had a red flag before the chequered flag (free tyre change and standing restart, not modelled); any car ran an intermediate or wet tyre (the simulator is dry-only); more than 5% of its laps have no stint data; or no data came back. Safety cars and virtual safety cars do not exclude a race: they are replayed.
